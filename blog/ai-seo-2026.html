How AI is Changing SEO in 2026 and What Agencies Need to Do Now

How AI is Reshaping SEO in 2026

Artificial intelligence has fundamentally changed how search engines work — and how SEO agencies need to operate. Understanding these shifts is no longer optional.

Google's AI Overview — What Changed

Google's AI Overviews now appear at the top of search results for millions of queries, answering questions directly without users clicking through to websites. This has reduced organic click-through rates on informational queries by 15–30%.

The implication: ranking is no longer enough. Your content needs to be cited by AI, not just ranked.

What Still Works — And Works Better

Authority Signals Are More Important Than Ever

Google's AI models are trained to trust authoritative sources. High-DA backlinks from real editorial sites are a core authority signal. Link building has not become less important — it has become the foundation.

E-E-A-T Has Become Non-Negotiable

Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trustworthiness. Google's quality raters now apply these criteria more strictly. Agencies need to ensure their clients' sites demonstrate genuine expertise.

How Agencies Need to Adapt

1. Focus on Transactional and Commercial Keywords

AI Overviews dominate informational queries. But for "buy", "hire", "best [service] near me" — traditional organic results still drive clicks.

2. Build Real Authority Through Quality Links

The sites Google cites in AI Overviews are the most authoritative in their niche. Quality backlinks from DA 50–90 sites remain the fastest path to that authority tier.

3. Add AI Automation to Your Service Stack

Agencies that can automate reporting, outreach, and content production will scale faster and at lower cost.

What This Means for Content Strategy

Content built purely to rank for a keyword is increasingly competing with AI Overviews that answer the query directly on the results page. Content built to demonstrate genuine expertise, backed by real credentials and real experience, is what AI models cite as a source.

This shifts the content brief. Instead of "write 1500 words targeting this keyword," the better brief is "write content that a genuine expert in this field would write, with specific data, examples, and a clear point of view."

The Agencies Losing Ground

Agencies still selling generic, templated content with no real expertise behind it are seeing the sharpest declines in AI Overview visibility. Google's systems are specifically trained to identify and deprioritize this kind of content in favor of sources demonstrating genuine authority.

Agencies that pair strong authority signals — real backlinks from real publishers — with genuinely expert content are seeing the opposite: increased visibility as AI systems look for sources worth citing.

Measuring Success Beyond Rankings

With AI Overviews absorbing a share of clicks that used to go to organic results, ranking position alone is an incomplete success metric. Agencies need to start tracking whether client content gets cited within AI-generated answers, alongside traditional ranking and traffic metrics. This is a newer discipline with less mature tooling than traditional rank tracking, but agencies that start measuring it now will have a real data advantage over competitors still reporting on rankings alone.

The Bottom Line

AI has raised the bar for SEO. The agencies that invest in real authority — genuine expertise, high-quality backlinks, and consistent content — will dominate the next decade.
