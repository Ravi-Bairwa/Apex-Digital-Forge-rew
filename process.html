Common Questions

About our process

What if I'm not happy with a placement?

Every placement is verified against the DA, traffic, and niche relevance criteria agreed before the campaign starts. If a specific placement doesn't meet that standard, we replace it at no extra cost — you're never asked to pay again for a link that didn't meet spec.

Can I request specific publisher sites?

Yes. If you have preferred sites within your niche, share them during onboarding and we'll check availability and pricing. Most clients are happy to let our team match sites based on the niche and DA criteria, since we already know which sites in our network perform best for each vertical.

How do I track progress mid-campaign?

Your live Google Sheet updates in real time as each placement goes live — no need to wait for a scheduled report. You can check it any time, and we also send a proactive update partway through every campaign so you're never left wondering where things stand.

What happens after the first campaign ends?

Most clients continue with a recurring monthly campaign once they see the first batch of links go live and rankings start responding. There's no auto-renewal or lock-in — you decide whether and when to scale up based on the results you're seeing.
