FAQ

Common questions

What is a lead generation system?
It is the connected set of steps that finds or attracts potential customers, captures their details, follows up, qualifies them and measures results, so lead flow does not depend on ad-hoc effort.
Do you promise a number of leads?
No. Results depend on your market, offer and sales process, and anyone guaranteeing a lead count is guessing. We build the system, report honestly on what it produces and refine it.
Is cold outreach compliant?
Rules differ by country and by audience. We build in opt-out handling and sensible practices, and you remain responsible for confirming what applies to your market.
Can you combine this with SEO?
Yes. Inbound traffic from SEO and GEO makes the system cheaper over time, and our existing services cover on-page, technical and off-page SEO.
How much does it cost?
It depends on your market, channels and how much of the system you need, so we quote after scoping. A capture-and-follow-up setup and a full outbound and inbound engine are very different projects.
