Choosing a Partner

Lead generation agency, in-house SDR, freelancer or list vendor?

Each option suits a different stage. Here is an honest comparison, including where we are not the best fit.

01
An in-house SDR

Best when: You have a repeatable offer and budget for ongoing management.

Watch out for: Ramp-up time, management effort and tooling costs.

02
A freelancer

Best when: A defined outreach campaign with a clear brief.

Watch out for: Availability and continuity, and inbound is often missing.

03
A list vendor

Best when: You only need contact data and have your own outreach process.

Watch out for: Data accuracy and deliverability if the list is used carelessly.

04
An agency like ours

Best when: You want outbound, inbound, follow-up and reporting built as one system.

Watch out for: It needs a clear offer to work with, and we do not guarantee lead numbers because results depend on your market.

FAQ

Common questions

What does a lead generation agency do?
It defines your target customer and offer, builds prospect lists, runs outreach, captures inbound enquiries, qualifies and routes leads and reports on results.
Do you guarantee a number of leads?
No. Results depend on your offer and market, and anyone guaranteeing a lead count is guessing. We build the system, report honestly and refine it.
Do you work with B2B, D2C and SaaS?
Yes. B2B, D2C and SaaS founders are our focus, and the system is shaped to your sales model.
Is cold outreach compliant?
Rules differ by country and audience. We build in opt-out handling and sensible practices, and you remain responsible for confirming what applies in your market.
How do I hire a lead generation agency?
Share your offer and market. We review them, propose the channels and system, and quote the work. Be cautious of agencies that cannot explain how they protect your sending reputation.
