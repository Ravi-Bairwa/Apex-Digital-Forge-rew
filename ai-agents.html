Pricing

AI agent development pricing: what drives the cost

We do not publish a price list, because two projects with the same name can differ many times over in effort. We quote after a discovery call, with a written scope, so you know what you are paying for before you commit. A small first project is a good way to start. These are the factors that move the price.

01
Scope of the job

An agent that answers common questions is far simpler than one that qualifies leads and books meetings.

02
Integrations

CRMs, calendars, inboxes and help desks each add work and testing.

03
Quality of your knowledge

Clear, current documents make a more accurate agent and a cheaper build.

04
Risk level

Agents touching payments, contracts or sensitive data need more safeguards.

05
Channels

A website widget, an inbox and a messaging app each need their own setup.

06
Running costs

Model usage and hosting are ongoing and separate from the build cost.

FAQ

Common questions

What is an AI agent?
An AI agent is software that takes a goal, decides on steps and uses tools such as your CRM, calendar or documents to complete a task, instead of just answering one message. It still needs limits and oversight.
Can an agent replace a team member?
Usually it takes over a slice of a role, such as first-pass lead qualification or answering routine questions, so people can focus on judgement and relationships. We scope agents to a narrow job on purpose.
What happens when the agent is wrong?
We design for that: answers grounded in your content, hand-off to a person when confidence is low, approval steps for sensitive actions and logs you can review. No agent is perfect, so we plan the safety net.
Which AI models and tools do you use?
We choose based on the task, cost, speed and your data requirements, and tell you which providers will handle your data before we build.
How much does an AI agent cost?
It depends on the job, the integrations and the deployment, so we quote after scoping. A single-purpose agent and a multi-agent workflow are very different projects.
How do I hire an AI agent development company?
Describe the job you want an agent to do and the tools it would use. We scope it, explain what it should and should not do, and quote the work. Ask any company how they handle mistakes, data and handover before you sign.
